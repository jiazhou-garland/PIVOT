Motivating Analysis

Temporal Pivot Localization Drives Hindsight Recovery

Zero-Gradient Silence and Coarse Credit

GRPO normalizes episode returns across N rollouts into one scalar advantage Anrl and applies it uniformly to every action token. Evaluating untrained Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct across five VAGEN tasks (N = 8) exposes two bottlenecks:

Takeaway Uniform failure returns (σ = 0) silence GRPO updates entirely; even when σ > 0, episode-level credit never isolates the first unrecoverable step t*.

Counterfactual Rollback Probes: Local Suffix Rollback over Full-Horizon Restart

To test what actually drives hindsight recovery in OPD/OPSD, we probe failed rollouts of untrained Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct along two axes: local suffix rollback at t* and full-horizon restart from t = 0 with diagnostic hints.

Regime 1 · Local Suffix Rollback

Restore simulator state to st*

Compares no-hint t* (pure reset), no-hint t*+1 (one step late), and diagnostic-self / teacher t* (appending πbase or Qwen3-VL-8B hints after resetting).

Regime 2 · Full-Horizon Restart

Restart from t = 0 without intermediate resets

Compares no-hint, generic planning advice, and diagnostic-self / teacher t0 (injecting the same pivot-grounded hints at turn 0 with no reset).

Formal definitions: pivot step t* & return lift rate ΔR

Empirical Findings

Finding 1 · Local Suffix

Exact timing governs local recovery

Resetting to t* with no-hint lifts ΔR by 3.3%–56.6%, while delaying one step (t*+1) collapses gains (PrimitiveSkill 18.4% → 8.7%; Sokoban 3.3% → 0.9%). Once restored, textual hints add little (≤ 2.2 points on most tasks).

Finding 2 · Full Horizon

Pivot diagnostics guide global restarts

Without resets (t = 0), pivot-grounded hints beat both no-hint and generic prompts on structured tasks (Sokoban 15.9% vs. 13.7% / 11.6%; PrimitiveSkill 44.7% vs. 40.2%). On open-ended Navigation, they still beat generic advice (48.6% vs. 45.1%).

Finding 3 · Silent Groups

Both regimes reopen GRPO signal

Both regimes cut σ = 0 group rates on sparse-reward tasks, dropping Sokoban from 48.3% to 32.8% and PrimitiveSkill from 16.5% to 0.5% (local-suffix probes: 37.8–41.7% and 3.8–6.0%).

Takeaway Local recovery depends strictly on exact timing at t* (making text redundant after a reset); without resets, pivot-grounded diagnostics outperform unguided restarts and generic prompts.

Resolving the Pivot Credit Assignment Dilemma via Visual Grounding

Pivot Credit Assignment Dilemma Local rollbacks at t* isolate recovery but require costly or unavailable simulator resets; full-horizon restarts avoid resets but waste rollouts on valid prefixes (t < t*) and dilute credit.

Can we embed pivot-grounded visual diagnostics directly into token-level updates without rollbacks or extra rollouts?

Principle I

Non-Invasive Pivot Grounding

Localize t* and diagnose failures directly from observable visual histories, with no hidden state access or resets.

Principle II

Counterfactual Credit Concentration

Convert localized visual hindsight into token-level updates on existing failed rollouts without re-sampling.

Principle III

Zero Test-Time Overhead

Internalize hindsight into policy weights so inference runs as a standalone Student with no extra models or prompts.

Method

PIVOT: On-Policy Self-Distillation via Pivot-Grounded Visual Hindsight

One shared policy πθ unifies Analyzer, Privileged Teacher, and Student during training, then ships strictly as an unprivileged Student with no rollbacks and no test-time overhead.

Roles

Role 1 · Diagnostic Analyzer

Reads the visual collage and action log to predict (t̂, m̂, r̂) without hidden state, and crops the 3-panel neighborhood Pt̂ = [ot̂−1, ot̂, ot̂+1].

Role 2 · Privileged Teacher

Detached (stop-gradient) evaluator that re-scores the Student’s original failed tokens under privileged hindsight h̃t = (p, ht), with no extra rollouts.

Role 3 · Acting Student

Acts on ordinary history ht and trains with GRPO + confidence-gated OPD on failed rollouts, suppressing high-risk pivot tokens even when σ = 0.

Stages

I

Stage I (SFT): Pivot Grounding

3-epoch SFT on offline diagnostic triplets (t*, m*, r*) from visual collages and action logs (959–1,719 trajectories per task) to initialize θsft.

II

Stage II (RL): Token Re-Weighting

250 RL updates per task (N = 8; N = 4 on PrimitiveSkill) from θsft, distilling visual-diagnostic hindsight onto failed tokens via gated OPD.

∅

Inference: Standalone Student

Strips Analyzer and Teacher; deploys purely as πθ(· | ht) with zero extra compute, parameters, or prompts.

Stage I (SFT): Non-Invasive Failure Diagnosis for Pivot Grounding

Each failed trajectory is labeled with a diagnostic triplet (t*, m*, r*) and paired strictly with observable inputs—a step-indexed visual collage C(τ), action sequence a0:T−1, and prompt q. Autoregressive SFT produces θsft with non-invasive pivot grounding.

Stage II (RL): Counterfactual Token Re-Weighting via Visual-Diagnostic Hindsight

On each failed rollout, the 3-panel visual crop Pt̂ = [ot̂−1, ot̂, ot̂+1] pairs with (m̂, r̂) to form privileged hindsight p. The detached Teacher re-scores the Student’s original failed tokens under (p, ht), suppressing high-risk pivot tokens while leaving valid prefixes intact—even when GRPO advantages vanish (σ = 0). At test time, only the standalone Student remains.

Experiments

Evaluation Across Five Multi-Turn VLM Benchmarks

Evaluated across cognitive grid puzzles, embodied 3D control, and generative SVG reconstruction on three backbones, with zero inference-time overhead.

Experimental setup (environments, baselines, variants & implementation)

Environments & Metrics

VAGEN suite across three paradigms: Cognitive Grid Puzzles (Sokoban, FrozenLake), Embodied 3D Control (Navigation, PrimitiveSkill), and Generative Reasoning (SVG). Puzzle and control tasks report Success Rate (SR); SVG reports composite DINO + DreamSim similarity.

Backbones & Reference Baselines

Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct, Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct, and Qwen3.5-VL-2B. Compared against Vanilla-GRPO, SFT-GRPO (θsft without OPD), reproduced gated-OPD baseline SDAR (frozen Qwen2.5-VL-7B teacher, episode-level skills, all valid tokens), and world-model SOTA (VAGEN, GLANCE).

PIVOT Variants & Ablation Arms

All start from θsft and vary Teacher context p: P (panel), M (mode), M+P, and M+P+R (full default with guidance skill), plus negative control M+P+Random+R with random pivot indices.

Implementation Details

Stage I runs 3-epoch SFT. Stage II runs 250 RL updates on every environment (including Sokoban), applying confidence-gated OPD (λ = 0.01, β = 5) on failed trajectories (all trajectories on SVG).

Main Results

On Qwen2.5-VL-3B, PIVOT reaches 0.90 overall (+8% over SFT-GRPO, +13% over Vanilla-GRPO), surpassing GLANCE (0.86) and SDAR (0.79) with the largest lifts on Sokoban (+16%) and PrimitiveSkill (+6%). On Qwen3-VL-2B it reaches 0.92 (+12%), and on Qwen3.5-VL-2B it reaches 0.91 (+6% over SFT-GRPO, +26% over Vanilla-GRPO).

Ablations

Diagnostic Context Components

Across both backbones, failure-mode text (M) provides a strong base, adding the localized visual panel (M+P) lifts planning and control (3B Sokoban 0.86 → 0.90, SVG 0.84; 2B Sokoban 0.82 → 0.97), and the full stack (M+P+R) is the most reliable default—best on four of five tasks for 3B (0.95 / 0.84 / 0.90 / 1.00) and best overall for 2B (0.92).

Sensitivity to Pivot Localization

Replacing predicted pivots with random step indices (M+P+Random+R) degrades every benchmark (0.75 Sokoban, 0.69 FrozenLake, 0.80 Navigation, 0.87 PrimitiveSkill, 0.74 SVG), confirming that unanchored visual context injects misleading supervision.

Training Dynamics and Efficiency

Self-Evolving Pivot Localization

Checked against FrozenLake’s shortest-path solver during RL, 15-step moving Analyzer accuracy (t̂ = t*) rises from ~0.56 to 0.90 by step 100 and holds near 0.95—forming a self-evolving loop that sharpens credit over training.

Trajectory Compression and Decision Efficiency

On Sokoban, both policies start near 8 turns. PIVOT drops below 4 turns within 120 updates and averages 3.4 turns over the final 50 steps versus 4.6 for Vanilla-GRPO, cutting deadlocks and redundant wandering.

End-to-End Training and Inference Efficiency

Shorter rollouts cut Sokoban step time from 102s to 95s. Including the 11-minute SFT warm-up, PIVOT finishes in 6.8h versus 7.1h for Vanilla-GRPO, and deploys with zero extra latency or prompt overhead.

Conclusion

Resolving the Pivot Credit Assignment Dilemma

Empirical Diagnosis

Pivot Credit Assignment Dilemma

Local suffix rollback requires exact timing at t* (with local text adding little) but costly resets; full-horizon restarts avoid resets and benefit from pivot diagnostics yet dilute prefix credit.

PIVOT Framework

Unified Three-Role Self-Distillation

One policy πθ acts as Analyzer, detached Teacher, and Student to distill 3-panel visual hindsight onto failed tokens—eliminating both training rollbacks and test-time overhead.

Performance & Efficiency

Self-Evolving Accuracy at Lower Cost

Reaches 0.90 on Qwen2.5-VL-3B (+8%, beating GLANCE 0.86), 0.92 on Qwen3-VL-2B (+12%), and 0.91 on Qwen3.5-VL-2B (+6%), with self-evolving pivot accuracy (0.56 → 0.95) and faster training (6.8h vs. 7.1h).
